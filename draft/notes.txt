Application Layer:

Instead of connecting Gemini with MCP servers directly
and business logic, we use an application layer that coordinates 
the messages between the two entites to be more secured and seperate in which
if we want to change an entity without changing the others

RAG is used to let gemini searching in the document for a specific keywords
and then generate a readable sentence to the user.

Firstly, it reads from a document (Loading), then dividing the sentences into small chunks (Chunking), converting
them into digital representation (Embedding) (Because if we compare the words, dialects or expression of the user 
can differ from what is written in the document). In embedding process, it converts the text into units called tokens that 
can be words or parts of words, then it processes those tokens into internal digital representations and then procesing them with neural
network according to what it learns, finally it generates the final vector of numbers that represents the text in multidimensional mathematical 
space.
Then searching for the most related words with the user questions (Retrieval). The user question is also converted into digital representation
and vector to enable the model to compare between the vectors and give the similarity percentage which is given by cosine similarity. 
Cosine similarity is a mathematical standard that used to identify how two vectors are identical by calculating the cosine, if the 
vectors are neat to each other, the angle between them is small and the percentage is near to 1, if they are orthognal 0, and if they are
in the opposite direction, the result is -1.

Finally it returns the related words to Gemini to generate a response
